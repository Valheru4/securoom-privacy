Privacy Policy for Securoom

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to Securoom. Your privacy and security are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information, data, and device permissions.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Securoom is designed with a local-first architecture:

  • Local Storage: All files, photos, passwords, or encrypted media stored inside Securoom remain exclusively on your device's local storage. We do not upload, transfer, or store your personal files on any external servers.
  • Biometric Data: Biometric authentication (fingerprint, face recognition) is handled natively by your device's operating system (Android BiometricPrompt). Securoom does not collect, store, or transmit your biometric identifiers.

2. Encryption

Securoom is designed with a local-first architecture:

  • Vault contents are stored on the device using secure encryption algorithms. The protection of your PIN and recovery information depends not only on the application's security mechanisms but also on your device's own screen lock and security measures.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Securoom uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob: Our application integrates Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous data such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and crash logs to personalize or measure ad performance. For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Device Permissions

To provide its security and utility features, Securoom may request access to:

  • Camera: Used exclusively for local security features (such as anti-intruder photo captures on wrong PIN attempts). Images captured are kept strictly local.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used solely to allow you to import, export, or back up your selected files within the app.
  • Biometrics: Used only to unlock your encrypted local vault.

5. Data Loss

In the event that the application is uninstalled, or the device is reset or wiped, locally encrypted data may be permanently lost.

6. Children's Privacy

Securoom does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: info@kesitek.com
